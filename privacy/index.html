Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Fee Sheet app for iPhone (“Fee Sheet” or “the app”) handles information.

Fee Sheet is developed and operated by Fatih Öztürkbay. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the developer. You can reach us using the details under Contact.

In short

  • Fee Sheet does not collect any data. We do not receive anything you enter in the app.
  • Your Buying Criteria, Seller Profiles, Saved Deals and preferences are stored only on your device.
  • There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server. The app makes no network requests.
  • Fee Sheet is a paid app with a one-time App Store price. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

1. Information we collect

None. Fee Sheet does not collect, transmit or share personal information or usage data. The app works entirely on your device, and what you enter is used only to calculate estimates on your screen and to keep the records you choose to save.

The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us when you contact support by email (see Contact).

2. Data stored on your device

Fee Sheet saves the following in the app’s local storage on your device, so that it is there the next time you open the app. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Data Fee Sheet stores on your device
CategoryWhat it contains
Buying CriteriaYour minimum profit and minimum ROI targets.
Seller ProfilesProfile names and the fee assumptions you enter for them, such as marketplace, advertising and payment processing percentages and fixed fees.
Saved DealsDeals you choose to save: the name you give them, the numbers you entered, the result at the time and the Buying Criteria used.
Onboarding progressWhich introductory tips and setup steps you have already seen or completed.
AppearanceYour choice of System, Light or Dark.

Numbers you type into the calculator are not stored unless you save the deal.

3. No accounts, network or analytics

Fee Sheet has no sign-in and no account system. The app does not connect to the internet: it makes no network requests and does not use a server of ours or anyone else’s.

The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software, and does not use your device’s advertising identifier. It does not ask for permission to track you.

Fee Sheet does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or other personal data on your device.

4. Purchasing the app

Fee Sheet is sold for a one-time price on the Apple App Store. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Your purchase is processed by Apple under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, Apple Account details or payment information. Apple provides developers with sales reports that do not identify individual buyers.

5. Device backups and new devices

Fee Sheet itself does not back up or sync your data. However, as with most iPhone apps, your device’s own backups (for example, iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer) may include the app’s data, depending on your settings. When you restore a device from such a backup or move to a new iPhone, iOS may restore that data. These backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us, and we cannot access them.

6. What we do not receive

We do not receive:

  • your deals, prices, costs, fees, profiles or settings;
  • your name, email address or contact details (unless you email us);
  • your payment details;
  • your location, device identifiers or usage data.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics and crash reports about the app. This is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by Fee Sheet.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. We cannot recover it for you. Device backups made earlier may still contain a copy, depending on your backup settings.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and keep reasonable records of it. You can ask us to delete them.

8. Children

Fee Sheet is not directed at children. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage on your device and is protected by iOS and your device security settings, such as your passcode. Because nothing is sent to us, there is no central copy of your data held by us.

10. Your choices

  • Delete individual records: you can delete Saved Deals and Seller Profiles in the app at any time.
  • Delete everything: delete the app from your device to remove all of its data.
  • Backups: manage iCloud Backup and other device backups in your iPhone’s settings.
  • Requests: depending on where you live, you may have rights under data protection laws, such as the right to access or delete personal information. Since the app collects none, this applies only to support emails. Contact us for any request.

11. This website

This support website’s own code sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking scripts, marketing pixels, external fonts or external scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; for information about how GitHub handles data, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, we will update this policy before that version is released. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
