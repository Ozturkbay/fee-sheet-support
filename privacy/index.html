Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Fee Sheet app for iPhone (“Fee Sheet” or “the app”) handles information.

Fee Sheet is developed and operated by Fatih Öztürkbay. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the developer. You can reach us using the details under Contact.

In short

  • Fee Sheet has no account, no server and no analytics of its own. We do not receive anything you enter in the app.
  • Your Buying Criteria, Seller Profiles, Saved Deals and preferences are stored only on your device.
  • Fee Sheet is free to download, and the free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google’s software in the app may collect data such as your IP address, device identifiers and ad interactions. See Advertising.
  • You choose how ads may use your data: on first use, Google’s consent form (where required by law) and Apple’s tracking prompt ask you. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are non-personalized or limited.
  • Fee Sheet Premium is an optional one-time in-app purchase that removes all ads. With Premium, the app makes no ad requests.

1. Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect personal information or usage data through the app. Fee Sheet has no account, no server and no analytics of our own. What you enter is used only to calculate estimates on your screen and to keep the records you choose to save on your device.

The free version of the app includes advertising software from Google: the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. This software may collect data from your device for advertising, analytics and consent purposes, as described in Advertising. That data is collected and processed by Google, not by us.

The only personal information we receive directly is what you choose to send us when you contact support by email (see Contact).

2. Data stored on your device

Fee Sheet saves the following on your device, in the app’s local storage or, for the Premium purchase record, in the iOS Keychain, so that it is there the next time you open the app. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Fee Sheet shows ads provided by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads are placed so that they stay out of the way of your work:

  • Full-screen (interstitial) ads appear only between tasks: after you save a deal, after you save a Seller Profile, or before Compare Profiles opens. They never appear while you are entering numbers or in the first 10 minutes after you first launch the app, and they are limited by spacing and a daily cap.
  • Rewarded ads play only when you choose to watch one, to unlock a Free limit for 24 hours: unlimited Saved Deals, or unlimited Seller Profiles together with comparing all profiles.
  • There are no banner ads. Ad content is limited to a maximum rating of PG.

If you have Fee Sheet Premium, no ads are shown, the app makes no ad SDK requests and the consent form is not shown.

4. Consent and tracking choices

On first use, after the welcome screen (and the quick tour, if you take it), the free version asks for your choices in this order:

  1. Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform), shown where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and US states with privacy regulations.
  2. Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, which asks whether Fee Sheet may track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites.

If you decline tracking or consent, you still see ads, but they are non-personalized or limited. The app’s features work the same either way.

You can change your choices at any time:

  • Tracking: in your iPhone’s Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent: where Google’s consent form applies, in the app at Settings → Ad privacy choices.

5. Accounts, analytics and network connections

Fee Sheet has no sign-in and no account system, and we do not run a server for the app. All calculations work offline.

The app connects to the internet only to:

  • load ads and the consent form from Google (free version only); and
  • communicate with the App Store when you buy or restore Fee Sheet Premium.

Fee Sheet contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software of our own. The Google SDKs described in Advertising include their own measurement and diagnostics.

Apart from Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, Fee Sheet does not ask for any permissions. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or other personal data on your device.

6. Fee Sheet Premium and purchases

Fee Sheet is free to download. Fee Sheet Premium is an optional, one-time (non-consumable) in-app purchase that removes all ads and all Free limits. It is not a subscription.

Purchases and Restore Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, Apple Account details or payment information. Apple provides developers with sales reports that do not identify individual buyers. To recognize your purchase offline, the app keeps the Premium purchase record described in section 2 on your device; it is not sent to us.

7. Device backups and new devices

Fee Sheet itself does not back up or sync your data. However, as with most iPhone apps, your device’s own backups (for example, iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer) may include the app’s data, depending on your settings. When you restore a device from such a backup or move to a new iPhone, iOS may restore that data. These backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us, and we cannot access them.

On a new iPhone, you can get Fee Sheet Premium back at no extra cost with Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account.

8. What we do not receive

We do not receive:

  • your deals, prices, costs, fees, profiles or settings;
  • your name, email address or contact details (unless you email us);
  • your payment details or Apple Account details;
  • your location, device identifiers or usage data in a form that identifies you. Google’s SDKs may collect such data for ads, as described in Advertising; AdMob reports to us only in aggregated form.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics and crash reports about the app. This is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by Fee Sheet.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its data from the device; iOS may keep the Premium purchase record in the Keychain. We cannot recover your data for you. Device backups made earlier may still contain a copy, depending on your backup settings.
  • Data collected by Google’s SDKs is kept by Google under its own policies (see Advertising).
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and keep reasonable records of it. You can ask us to delete them.

10. Children

Fee Sheet is not directed at children, and ad requests are not marked for child-directed treatment. Ad content is limited to a maximum rating of PG. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage on your device, and the Premium purchase record in the iOS Keychain. Both are protected by iOS and your device security settings, such as your passcode. Because nothing you enter is sent to us, there is no central copy of your data held by us.

12. Your choices

  • Tracking: allow or stop tracking at any time in your iPhone’s Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent: where Google’s consent form applies, review or change your choices in the app at Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • No ads: buy Fee Sheet Premium to remove all ads and stop all ad requests.
  • Delete individual records: you can delete Saved Deals and Seller Profiles in the app at any time.
  • Delete everything: delete the app from your device to remove its data.
  • Backups: manage iCloud Backup and other device backups in your iPhone’s settings.
  • Requests: depending on where you live, you may have rights under data protection laws, such as the right to access or delete personal information. We hold no data from the app, so requests to us apply to support emails. For data collected by Google’s SDKs, see the Google Privacy Policy. Contact us for any request.

13. This website

This support website’s own code sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking scripts, marketing pixels, external fonts or external scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; for information about how GitHub handles data, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

14. Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, we will update this policy before that version is released. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
