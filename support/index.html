About Fee Sheet

Fee Sheet is a resale profit calculator for iPhone. It answers two sourcing questions: should I buy this item to resell? and what is the minimum price I need to sell it for?

Everything works offline. There is no account to create, and your data is stored only on your device.

Check Deal: BUY or SKIP

On the calculator, choose Check Deal. Enter the purchase price, the expected sale price and your costs and fees. Fee Sheet shows the estimated net profit, ROI, margin, Break-even Sale Price and Maximum Buy Price.

The result is BUY only when the deal meets both of your Buying Criteria: the minimum profit and the minimum ROI. If either one is missed, the result is SKIP.

  • ROI is net profit divided by purchase price. If you pay $20 and make $20 profit, ROI is 100%.
  • Margin is net profit as a percentage of total gross revenue (sale price plus shipping charged to the buyer).
  • Break-even Sale Price is the lowest item price that avoids a loss with the current costs and fees.

ROI shows “—” when the purchase price is zero, and margin shows “—” when gross revenue is zero.

Target Sale

Choose Target Sale to work backwards: enter what you pay and your costs, and Fee Sheet finds the minimum item sale price that meets both your minimum profit and minimum ROI.

Shipping charged to the buyer is kept separate from the recommended item price. Tap Use this price to copy the price into Check Deal and review the full result.

Target Sale can’t be calculated when the combined percentage fees are 100% or more.

Maximum Buy Price

The Maximum Buy Price (shown under BUY LIMIT) is the most you can pay for an item while still meeting your current minimum profit and ROI, based on the sale price, costs and fees you entered. It is shown whether the result is BUY or SKIP.

A Maximum Buy Price of $0.00 means that no positive purchase price meets both targets at that sale price.

Buying Criteria

Your Buying Criteria are the targets used for BUY or SKIP, Target Sale and Maximum Buy Price. Set them in Settings:

  • Minimum Profit — default $15.
  • Minimum ROI — default 50%.

Tap Save settings to apply them. Changes apply to new calculations; Saved Deals keep the criteria that were used when they were saved.

Entering fees

Fee Sheet does not know any marketplace’s official fees and does not download fee tables. You enter your own fees, based on the marketplace and account you sell with:

  • Percentage fees — marketplace fee, advertising fee and payment processing fee. Each is applied to gross revenue: the sale price plus shipping charged to the buyer.
  • Fixed fees — fixed marketplace fee and payment processing fixed fee, in dollars per sale.
  • Other costs — shipping cost, packaging and supplies, and any other cost of the sale.

Marketplace fees vary by category, account and region, and they change over time. Check your marketplace’s current fee information and update your numbers when it changes.

Results are estimates. They are only as accurate as the numbers you enter. Fee Sheet does not guarantee any profit and is not financial, tax or accounting advice.

Seller Profiles

A Seller Profile saves a set of fee assumptions, such as the fees for one of your selling accounts, so you don’t have to enter them every time.

  • Create: go to Settings → Seller Profiles → Add Profile, give it a name and enter its fees.
  • Use: in the calculator, choose a profile under Seller Profile. Choose Custom / Manual to type fees for a single calculation.
  • Edit or delete: open the profile in Settings → Seller Profiles.

Compare Profiles

With at least two Seller Profiles, tap Compare Profiles in Check Deal to see the same deal with each profile’s fees. BEST PROFIT marks the profile with the highest estimated net profit. Comparisons use your current Buying Criteria and are not saved.

Saved Deals

After checking a deal, tap Save Deal and give it a name. Open your list with Saved on the calculator.

A Saved Deal is a snapshot: it keeps the numbers you entered, the result and the Buying Criteria at the time it was saved. Editing a Seller Profile or changing your Buying Criteria later does not change it.

Recalculate with current settings loads the deal into Check Deal using your current Buying Criteria. The original Saved Deal is not overwritten; save the new result as a separate deal if you want to keep it.

To remove a Saved Deal, open it and delete it. This can’t be undone.

Appearance

Go to Settings → Appearance and choose System, Light or Dark. System is the default and follows your iPhone’s light or dark setting.

Help & Quick Start

Open Settings → Help & Quick Start for a short guide and plain-English definitions of the financial terms. Tap Try a sample deal there to explore the calculator with example numbers. Sample values are for practice and are not saved.

The first time you open Fee Sheet, Show me how it works starts a short guided tour that highlights where to enter prices and fees and where to read the result. You can skip it at any step, and run it again any time from Help & Quick Start → Replay quick tour.

Price and purchases

Fee Sheet is a paid app with a one-time price on the App Store. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no ads.

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple. We can’t issue refunds ourselves; to request one, use Apple’s Report a Problem page.

New or replacement iPhone

You can download Fee Sheet again at no extra cost on any iPhone signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Find it in the App Store under your purchased apps.

Your data does not transfer automatically. Fee Sheet has no account or cloud sync. Your Buying Criteria, Seller Profiles and Saved Deals move to a new iPhone only if you restore it from a device backup (such as iCloud Backup) or transfer your data from your old iPhone during setup, and the backup includes the app’s data.

Deleting the app deletes its data from your device. If you want to keep your Saved Deals and Seller Profiles, don’t delete the app before your data has been moved or backed up.

Privacy

Fee Sheet collects no data. It has no account, analytics, ads or tracking and makes no network requests. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Troubleshooting

If a result looks wrong, check the inputs first:

  1. Check the selected Seller Profile. Make sure the right profile, or Custom / Manual, is selected.
  2. Check percentages and fixed fees. Enter percentages as percentages (for example, 13.25 for 13.25%), and fixed fees in dollars.
  3. Check shipping. Shipping charged to the buyer adds to revenue; shipping cost is what you pay to send the item.
  4. Check your Buying Criteria in Settings if a result is SKIP when you expected BUY.

If the app itself isn’t working as expected:

  1. Close and reopen the app from the app switcher. Saved data is kept.
  2. Restart your iPhone.
  3. Update the app from the App Store when an update is available.
  4. Contact us if the problem continues — see below.

Please don’t delete the app as a troubleshooting step unless we suggest it: your data is stored only on your device.

Contact support

  • a screenshot, if it helps.
